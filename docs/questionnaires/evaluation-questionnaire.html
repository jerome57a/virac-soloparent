e-SoloParentID: An Online Application and Renewal System with Automated Verification and SMS Notification

User Evaluation Questionnaire

About this survey. We are testing a web system that lets Solo Parents apply for or renew their Solo Parent ID online, and lets MSWDO Virac staff review applications. We would like your honest opinion. There are no right or wrong answers, and the survey takes about 10 minutes.

Your answers are used only for this study and are reported as group results. You do not need to write your name. Taking part is voluntary and you may stop at any time.

Para sa mga Pilipinong respondent: Sinusuri namin ang isang website na ginagamit sa pag-apply o pag-renew ng Solo Parent ID. Mangyaring sagutin nang tapat. Walang tama o maling sagot. Kusang-loob ang pakikilahok at hindi kailangang isulat ang inyong pangalan.

Researcher sheet (remove before printing for respondents)

Who to ask

At least 15 to 30 respondents: Solo Parent applicants (and a few who were not comfortable with computers), the MSWD staff who review applications, an administrator, and 3 to 5 IT experts. State the actual numbers in your chapter.

Scoring SUS (Part 2)

  1. For odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): score = answer - 1.
  2. For even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): score = 5 - answer.
  3. Add the ten scores and multiply by 2.5. The result is 0 to 100 for each respondent.
  4. Average across respondents. About 68 is the usual benchmark for "average" usability; 80 and above is commonly read as good to excellent.

Scoring Part 3

Ignore N/A. Compute the mean of each statement and of each section (A to F), and an overall mean. Sections A and B together give the mean for functional suitability; C is performance efficiency, D reliability, E security and F interaction capability. A common reading of the 5-point scale:

MeanInterpretation
4.21 - 5.00Strongly agree / Excellent
3.41 - 4.20Agree / Good
2.61 - 3.40Neutral / Fair
1.81 - 2.60Disagree / Poor
1.00 - 1.80Strongly disagree / Very poor

Report applicants, staff and IT experts separately as well as combined: their opinions of interaction capability and of technical quality often differ.

Notes

  • Items B2 and B3 apply to staff only; B1 to applicants. Mark the others N/A.
  • The Filipino wording of the SUS statements is a plain-language adaptation, not an official translation. Have a Filipino-language teacher or adviser review it, and say so in your chapter, before you use it.
  • ISO/IEC 25010:2023 has nine characteristics. This study rates the five named in its objectives: functional suitability, performance efficiency, reliability, security and interaction capability (the 2011 edition called it usability; the SUS in Part 2 measures it too). Compatibility, maintainability, flexibility and safety are not rated. If your panel asks for them, add items for IT experts only; applicants cannot judge them.
  • Keep completed forms private. Do not collect names unless your consent form requires it.
